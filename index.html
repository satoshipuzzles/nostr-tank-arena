Nostr Tank Arena

Four-player deathmatch with no game server. Your npub is your identity, relays are the netcode, your score is a signed event.

Controllers
No gamepad detected. Plug one in or press a button on it.

Browsers only reveal a controller after you press something on it — that is the pairing step, and there is nothing for this game to configure. Left stick drives, right stick aims, right trigger or A fires. Four pads on one machine all work; each one drives the tank of whoever is signed in on that tab.

Relays

One websocket URL per line. Shells and positions ride on ephemeral events, so relays forward them and store nothing. A relay you control will always feel better than a busy public one.

Guest play generates a throwaway key in this tab. Nothing to install, nothing saved.

Leaderboard

Self-reported and signed by each player. Anyone can publish any number — see How to cheat this in the README.
